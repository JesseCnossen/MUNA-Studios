# Privacy Policy

**MUNA Studios Discord Bot**
Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the MUNA Studios Discord bot ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By adding the Bot to a server or interacting with it (including by direct message), you agree to this policy.

## 1. Who we are

The Bot is operated by MUNA Studios, run by Jesse Cnossen, based in the Netherlands.

Contact: **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), MUNA Studios is the data controller for the personal data described below.

## 2. Information we collect

The Bot only receives the information Discord makes available to it through the Discord API. Depending on which features are used, this may include:

- **Discord identifiers:** your user ID, username and display name, avatar hash, and the IDs of servers (guilds), channels and roles the Bot interacts with.
- **Message content:** the content of commands you send to the Bot, messages that mention or reply to the Bot, and direct messages you send to it. The Bot does not store the content of other messages in a server unless a specific feature requires it and says so.
- **Server settings:** configuration saved by server administrators, such as allowed channels, roles or preferences.
- **Technical and log data:** timestamps, command names, and error logs needed to keep the Bot running and diagnose problems.

We do **not** knowingly collect passwords, payment information, or government identifiers, and we do not ask you to send them to the Bot. Please don't.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information to:

- operate the Bot and respond to your commands and messages;
- store and apply server and user settings;
- prevent abuse, enforce our Terms of Service, and keep the Bot secure;
- debug problems and improve the Bot.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on:

- **Performance of a service you request** (Art. 6(1)(b)), to respond to your commands;
- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)), to keep the Bot secure, prevent abuse and fix bugs;
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)), where we must retain or disclose data by law.

## 5. Sharing

We share information only:

- with **Discord**, because the Bot runs on Discord's platform and is subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy);
- with **service providers** that host the Bot or its data on our behalf (hosting, database, logging), under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection terms;
- when **required by law**, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the public or MUNA Studios.

Server administrators can see settings they configure for their own server.

## 6. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above:

- server settings are kept while the Bot remains in the server and are deleted within a reasonable period after it is removed;
- logs are kept for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) unless needed to investigate abuse or a security incident;
- data you ask us to delete is removed as described in section 8.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data, such as restricted access and keeping the Bot's credentials secret. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise these rights, contact us at **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**. We may need to verify your identity first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

You can also stop all data collection by removing the Bot from your server and not messaging it.

## 9. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use Discord (13, or higher in some countries). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. International transfers

Discord and our hosting providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
